Why we're building this

a short note, not a pitch

We've both sat in the support queue. The best person on the team was never the one who'd read the most docs. It was the one who remembered that one customer, knew which rule had quietly changed, and knew who to call when payroll was due in the morning.

When companies brought in AI agents, that person's knowledge was the first thing to go missing. The agents were polite and fast, and wrong in ways that cost real customers real money.

Tacit is our attempt to write down what those people know. Their names stay on it, a human confirms every change, and the agent is honest when it doesn't know. We'd love to show you what your team knows.

Built to be trusted with your tickets

Support data is sensitive. Here's exactly what Tacit does with yours.

Read-only

Tacit never changes anything in your helpdesk. A Zendesk API token is used once for the import and never saved.

People approve

Every procedure has a named owner. When the team's behavior changes, Tacit asks before it updates anything.

Your data, your model calls

Questions Heads of CX ask us

Something missing? Book a pilot below and we'll talk it through.

What do I need to get started?

An export of resolved tickets that includes internal notes, or a Zendesk API token. Most teams upload a CSV and see their first procedures in under a minute.

How accurate is it?

Every procedure comes with its evidence and a confidence score. A rule is kept only if at least two tickets support it. Below 60% confidence, the agent hands the ticket to a person instead of guessing, and an owner confirms each procedure before you rely on it.

Does it replace our support agent or helpdesk?

No. Tacit sits beside them. It gives your existing AI agent the knowledge it's missing, as a skills file or JSON it can load, and keeps that knowledge current.

What happens when our process changes?

Tacit checks each new ticket against what it knows. When people start doing something differently, it flags the change, works out what the new rule is and when it started, and asks the owner to confirm.

Which helpdesks work?

Zendesk works through a live, read-only import. Any other helpdesk works with a CSV export that has the customer's message and the agent's notes. Intercom and Freshdesk connectors are on the roadmap.

What does it cost?

The knowledge audit is free. After that it's $3,000 to $15,000 a month depending on ticket volume and sources, and most teams compare that with the value of the extra tickets their agent resolves.

    The format agents run

    What your team knows, written so an agent can follow it and a person can still read it. It exports as a skills file and as portable JSON, and we plan to publish the format openly so it isn't locked into one vendor.
