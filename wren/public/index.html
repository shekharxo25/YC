Privacy and safety

Your child's voice is not our product

We built Wren around the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) from the first line of code. We didn't add it later.

A parent says yes first

Only an adult can create an account. We ask for your consent before your child reads a single word.

Audio isn't kept

Speech is turned into words and then thrown away. We keep recordings only if you opt in, and you can change that at any time.

No ads, ever

No ads, no third-party trackers and no selling data. The only people who pay us are parents.

Take your data with you

Download everything we hold about your family as a file, whenever you like.

Delete means delete

One button erases your account, your children's profiles and every session.

A grown-up gate

Leaving kid mode takes a press-and-hold that only grown-ups can do, so settings and billing stay out of reach.

Questions

What parents ask us

What age is Wren for?

Ages 4 to 8. Right now it focuses on the step from phonics to reading, which is where most 4 to 6 year olds are. Writing and arithmetic come next, using the same engine that listens and adapts.

Does it really understand a five-year-old's speech?

Children's speech is the hard case, because it's high-pitched, full of pauses and often mispronounced. Wren combines speech recognition with the text your child is supposed to be reading. That way it's judging "did they read stop?" and not trying to transcribe open-ended speech. If the microphone isn't a good fit, a grown-up can tap the words that were tricky instead.

My child was flagged at a school reading screening. Is this for us?

Yes. Many families start there. The free reading check gives you something concrete to bring to the teacher: the specific sounds and patterns your child hasn't secured yet. Wren supports what school is doing. It doesn't replace a teacher or a specialist evaluation, and if you're worried about dyslexia, please also talk to your school or pediatrician.

How much screen time is this?

About fifteen minutes a day, and every session has a clear end. There's no autoplay, no endless feed and nothing to buy inside the app. Most of that time your child is reading out loud, not tapping.

What do you do with my child's voice?

By default, audio is turned into words and then discarded. We only keep recordings if you opt in to help improve accuracy for kids' voices, and you can turn that off at any time. You can download or delete all your family's data from your account.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel in two taps from your account, and there's no phone call. If you cancel during the 7-day trial, you won't be charged.

Do you work with schools?

Not yet. We're starting with families. Please tell your child's teacher about us. We're planning classroom pilots for later.
